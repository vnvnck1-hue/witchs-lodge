### 최종 프롬프트

Use case: stylized-concept.
Design ONE COMMON BASE CARD TEMPLATE for the game 마녀산장. This is the everyday standard card format for dozens of ordinary mercenary, sheep-wool, wood, and creature cards. The supplied image is ONLY a reference for the axe mercenary's face, chest-up framing and ink illustration style. Completely replace its ornate template.

CRITICAL ART DIRECTION: modest, simple, everyday, practical, reproducible. Character artwork is the focal point. The template is made of a plain rounded rectangle, simple panels and chunky ink lines. No themed collector treatment. ZERO decorative emblems, moons, stars, vines, branches, snow on border, wax seals, ropes, bolts, wood grain, carved signs, gold, metallic edging, filigree, black curling shapes, scroll ribbons, torn paper, intricate frames, rarity symbols or glow. The card border must be neutral and equally appropriate for a resource card or a person card. Preserve a little handmade charm in linework and restrained faint paper grain; avoid corporate UI slickness, harsh digital gradients or photorealistic material.

One single upright 2:3 portrait card shown face-on, all corners visible, a narrow near-black background outside the card. Original dark-bearded axe mercenary, orange winter hood, olive scarf, axe at shoulder, large face and upper chest only, simplified teal snowy forest in artwork only. No lower body. Keep character identity, bold black flat-color storybook print illustration. Face prominent and large, no frame overlap obscuring face or text. Use muted plum gray, warm off-white and near-black for template, with character orange and forest teal within the illustration. Art should occupy roughly 65% of card area. Compact name and category at the TOP so cards remain identifiable in an overlapping hand; rules panel at bottom, readable, generous padding. No costs, badges, stats or other information.

Only exact Korean text:
Title "도끼 용병"
Category "용병 · 일회 계약"
Rules "나무에 사용하면 벌목합니다." then "작업을 마치면 귀가합니다."
Title bold handmade Korean lettering, rules simple legible Korean lettering. Lettering moderate sized, not gigantic ceremonial calligraphy. No additional writing, no watermark, no logos.
This is an actual polished painted concept for a BASIC card format, not a wireframe or multi-card sheet.

LAYOUT A — PLAIN SOLID FRAME. An even-width muted plum-gray rounded outer frame, approximately 4% of card width, with a single thick near-black contour. A plain off-white rectangular header at top, title left aligned and category on small second line. Large rectangular art window directly underneath, squared edges with mildly rounded corners, separated from header by just one black horizontal line. Bottom off-white rectangular rules panel, two centered lines, separated by one black horizontal line. Header, art, rules share the same aligned inner width. No nested borders, no ornament, no tabs, no colored category strip. Clear sturdy three-section ordinary card.
